## 🇨🇳 中文

把想法、大纲、分集计划、草稿或现有剧本，变成结构可控的中文剧本：长剧约 24-40 集、每集 30-45 分钟，中剧约 20-30 集、每集 5-15 分钟，短剧约 30-100 集、每集 1-3 分钟。可推导的技术事实自动锁定，只对会改变剧情、关系、结局、总集数、剧型、单集时长或输出形态的阻塞性问题提问；叙事方式这类创作决策则用弹窗给出首选推荐与 4-6 个备选。全流程用 decision_lock 与分阶段连锁校验，覆盖三幕或变体结构、多线叙事规划、钩子/悬念/爽点、伏笔与道具追踪、人物体系、逐集写作以及剧本优化与诊断。只做剧本与结构，不产出成片、资产、分镜图、配音或剪辑。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入想法/大纲/分集计划/草稿 → 输出分集大纲、结构表或完整单集剧本
- 按剧型自动推导总集数、单集时长、幕边界与锚点集
- 叙事方式弹窗：首选推荐 + 理由 + 4-6 个备选，选定后写入 decision_lock
- 钩子、悬念、爽点、付费卡点与伏笔/道具追踪成体系设计
- 每个阶段跑连锁校验，防止后续剧本悄悄改变叙事模式
- 支持创作、扩写、优化、诊断、改写与规范化，可按要求逐字保留原对白

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns an idea, outline, episode plan, draft or existing scripts into a structurally controlled Chinese drama script: long-form at about 24-40 episodes of 30-45 minutes, medium-form at about 20-30 episodes of 5-15 minutes, or short-form at about 30-100 episodes of 1-3 minutes. Derivative technical facts are locked automatically, and it asks only blocking unknowns - those that would change plot, relationships, ending, total episodes, drama type, episode length or output shape. Creative calls such as narrative method come through a popup with a recommended option and 4-6 alternatives. A decision lock plus staged chain QC covers three-act or variant structures, multi-line planning, hooks, suspense and payoffs, foreshadowing and prop tracking, character systems, episode-by-episode writing and script diagnosis. Script and structure only: no finished video, assets, storyboard images, voice or editing.

### ✨ What it does

- An idea, outline, episode plan or draft in → an episode outline, structure table or full single-episode script out
- Derives total episodes, per-episode duration, act boundaries and anchor episodes from the drama type
- Narrative-method popup with a recommended option, its reason and 4-6 alternatives; the choice is written into the decision lock
- Designs hooks, suspense, payoff beats and paid-card points together with foreshadowing and prop tracking
- Runs chain QC at every stage so later scripts cannot silently change the storytelling mode
- Covers create, expand, optimise, diagnose, rewrite and standardise, preserving existing dialogue verbatim on request
