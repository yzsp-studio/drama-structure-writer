# Episode Templates

## Medium-Form 7-Node Template

Use for 5-15 minute medium-form episodes:

1. Nuclear hook: first 10-15 seconds, scaled by duration.
2. Fast opening + first爽点: first 10% of runtime.
3. Dense middle: about 60%; at least 3 narrative lines when duration allows.
4. Character / emotion development: embedded inside the middle.
5. Secondary escalation: about 10%.
6. Nuclear ending: about 15%.
7. Triple hook: final 3%; suspense + emotion + next爽点 preview.

## Long-Form Episode Template

Use for 30-45 minute episodes:

1. Cold open or emotional hook.
2. Previous-conflict continuation.
3. Main plot scene chain.
4. Subplot / relationship / antagonist movement.
5. Mid-episode turn.
6. Emotional or thematic scene.
7. Main conflict escalation.
8. End beat or cliffhanger.

Long-form episodes may slow down, but every scene must change information, relationship, power, or emotion.

## Short-Form Episode Template

Use for 1-3 minute episodes:

1. 0-3 seconds: direct conflict or shock hook.
2. 3-20 seconds: identity / situation / pressure made clear.
3. Middle: one focused conflict only; avoid too many lines.
4. Fast reversal or爽点.
5. Final 5-15 seconds: unresolved cliffhanger or paid-point.

Short-form episodes should not attempt full medium-form multi-line expansion unless the duration is close to 3 minutes and the user confirms it.

## Scaling Rule

Calculate seconds from percentages, then check readability. If dialogue cannot fit naturally, ask whether to reduce dialogue, split episode, or increase duration.
