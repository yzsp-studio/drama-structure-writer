# Drama Type Specifications

## Long-Form Drama

- Typical total episodes: 24-40.
- Typical episode duration: 30-45 minutes.
- Narrative feature: slower escalation, richer subplots, ensemble arcs, multi-stage emotional payoff.
- Structure tendency: three-act or five-phase long arc.
- Single episode: can include multiple complete scenes, quieter emotional scenes, and long-form subplot progression.

## Medium-Form Drama

- Typical total episodes: 20-30.
- Typical episode duration: 5-15 minutes.
- Narrative feature: combines fast hooks with enough space for multi-line conflict.
- Structure tendency: three-act structure works well.
- Single episode: the original 7-node template is the main reference, but timing must scale to actual duration.

## Short-Form Drama

- Typical total episodes: 30-100.
- Typical episode duration: 1-3 minutes.
- Narrative feature: very high hook density, fast conflict escalation, frequent cliffhangers, platform retention priority.
- Structure tendency: phase-based serialized hooks; paid-point / reversal cadence is more important than long scene depth.
- Single episode: must open within 3 seconds, escalate quickly, and end with a strong unresolved hook.

## Decision Rule

If drama type, total episodes, or single-episode duration is missing, use popup questions. Recommended defaults:

- long-form: 32 episodes × 40 minutes;
- medium-form: 24 episodes × 10 minutes;
- short-form: 60 episodes × 2 minutes.

Only apply these defaults after user authorization.
